Canopy LSP Demo
================

Try these features:
- Hover (K) over any word to see semantic analysis
- Code Actions (<leader>ca) on diagnostics
- Toggle inlay hints (<leader>ih)
- Navigate diagnostics ([d and ]d)

Simple Sentences
----------------

The cat runs.

John walks quickly.

Transitive Verbs (Agent + Patient)
----------------------------------

The dog chased the cat.

Mary read the book carefully.

The programmer fixed the bug.

Ditransitive Verbs (Agent + Recipient + Theme)
----------------------------------------------

John gave Mary a book.

The teacher sent the students an email.

She told him the truth.

Embedded Clauses
----------------

She believes that he left.

John knows that Mary likes coffee.

The scientist discovered that the theory was correct.

Complex Sentences
-----------------

After the rain stopped, the children played outside.

The book that John read was interesting.

Mary, who loves linguistics, studies theta roles.
